Azimuth 0° = front, +90° = right, ±180° = behind. Elevation +90° = straight up. Distance 0.3–10 m.

HRTF: the browser's generic head-related transfer functions (ITD + ILD + pinna spectral cues), so elevation and front/back work — but with a non-personal HRTF expect some up/down and front/back confusions.

ITD/ILD: only the two binaural difference cues (Woodworth spherical-head ITD + broadband ILD). They only encode lateral angle, so every point on the dashed ring (cone of confusion) sounds the same.

Distance: level (1/r), direct-to-reverberant ratio, air absorption (high-frequency loss far away), and in ITD/ILD mode a near-field ILD boost under 1 m.
